Pasos del flujo

  • Crear payment intent
    POST /api/payments/create — estado requires_confirmation
  • Listar métodos
    GET /api/payments/methods/list
  • Consultar pago
    GET /api/payments/:id
  • Confirmar pago
    POST /api/payments/:id/confirm — estado succeeded
  • Reembolsar
    POST /api/payments/:id/refund — crea reembolso rf_...
  • Cancelar
    POST /api/payments/:id/cancel — estado cancelled
